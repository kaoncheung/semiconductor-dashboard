💧 水資源｜五大科學園區比較Water Resources | Five Science Parks Comparison

🗂️ 八月研究焦點：竹科・中科・南科・嘉科・高雄科（F22）水資源來源與使用狀況August Research Focus: Water resource sources and usage across 5 science parks
🇹🇼 竹科（新竹）
竹科水資源：石門水庫＋寶山水庫＋寶二水庫為主，無大型主力水庫，先天最脆弱HSP Water: Shihmen + Baoshan + Baoer Reservoirs; No Major Primary Reservoir; Most Vulnerable by Design
竹科主要水源為石門水庫（跨區調度，桃園→新竹）、寶山水庫、寶二水庫。三座水庫總容量相對小，無法完全因應未來需求。新增水源包含南寮海淡廠（2028，10萬噸/日）、石門至新竹聯通管（2028，增加30萬噸/日調度能力）。2026年3月曾亮黃燈，梅雨後寶山寶二回復滿庫。寶山二期4座2nm廠每日用水估9.8萬噸，佔新竹總用水16–18%，農業用水衝突最激烈。HSP's primary water sources are Shihmen Reservoir (cross-region transfer from Taoyuan), Baoshan, and Baoer Reservoirs. Combined capacity is insufficient for future demand. New sources: Nanliao desalination plant (2028, 100K tons/day) and Shihmen-Hsinchu transfer pipeline (2028, +300K tons/day capacity). Yellow alert in March 2026; reservoirs returned to 100% after May plum rains. Four 2nm fabs estimated at 98,000 tons/day, representing 16–18% of Hsinchu's total—the most intense agricultural water conflict zone.
持續追蹤 來源：環境權保障基金會 | 聯合新聞網 | 經濟部聯通管工程
🇹🇼 中科（台中/后里）
中科水資源：鯉魚潭水庫＋石岡壩雙主力，水湳再生水廠2024年底供水1萬噸/日CIP Water: Liyutan + Shihgang Dam as Dual Sources; Shuinan Reclaimed Water Plant Supplying 10K Tons/Day Since Late 2024
中科台中園區主要水源為鯉魚潭水庫（大安溪，日供水70萬噸）與石岡壩（大甲溪，日供水70萬噸），兩者合計支應大台中地區140萬噸/日需求。台灣首例三合一再生水計畫（台中市水湳再生水廠）：污水+中水+再生水系統，2024年底起每日供水1萬噸至中科台中園區，MBR+RO雙重過濾接近電子級水質。中科后里基地另有農業用水衝突歷史——中科三期環評引發農民抗議，擔心大安溪、大甲溪農業用水被排擠。CIP's primary sources are Liyutan Reservoir (Da'an River, 700K tons/day capacity) and Shihgang Dam (Dajia River, 700K tons/day), jointly supplying the 1.4M tons/day greater Taichung demand. Taiwan's first triple-integrated reclaimed water project (Taichung Shuinan) uses MBR+RO dual filtration for near-electronic-grade quality, supplying 10,000 tons/day to CIP since late 2024. CIP Houli also has a history of agricultural water conflict—Phase 3 EIA triggered protests from farmers worried about Da'an and Dajia river irrigation being crowded out.
2024–2026 來源：台中市水利局（水湳再生水） | 公視「我們的島」（中科農業搶水）
🇹🇼 南科（台南）
南科水資源：曾文＋南化水庫為主，全台再生水最成熟基地，永康廠每日供6.7萬噸STSP Water: Tsengwen + Nanhua as Primary; Taiwan's Most Mature Reclaimed Water Site; Yongkang Plant Supplies 67K Tons/Day
南科主要水源為曾文水庫、南化水庫（兩者聯通管2024年完工）、烏山頭水庫、高屏堰調度。全台半導體業再生水最成熟基地：永康水資源回收中心（MBR+RO，9.5公里地下管線）已全面供水，每日供應台積電、聯電等6.7萬噸再生水，自來水使用量降低三成。台南海淡廠（全期20萬噸/日）規劃中。南科每日用水已達17萬噸，水利署預估2026年達32.5萬噸。STSP's primary sources are Tsengwen and Nanhua Reservoirs (connected via 2024-completed pipeline), Wushantou Reservoir, and Gaoping Weir transfers. Taiwan's most mature semiconductor reclaimed water site: Yongkang Water Reclamation Center (MBR+RO, 9.5km underground pipeline) now fully operational, supplying TSMC and UMC 67,000 tons/day of reclaimed water, cutting potable water use by 30%. Tainan desalination plant (full capacity 200K tons/day) is in planning. Current daily usage: 170K tons; WRA projects 325K tons/day by 2026.
2024–2026 來源：經濟日報（永康再生水廠直擊） | 中時（南科用水達17萬噸）
🇹🇼 嘉科（嘉義）
嘉科水資源：蘭潭＋仁義潭水庫為主，南化水庫可調度，新興園區水資源基礎設施尚待完善Chiayi Science Park Water: Lantan + Renyitan as Primary; Nanhua Dispatch Available; New Park's Water Infrastructure Still Developing
嘉義科學園區（嘉科）為台灣最新的科學園區，主要水源為蘭潭水庫與仁義潭水庫（嘉義市及嘉義縣主要水庫），另可由南化水庫調度支援。目前嘉科第一廠已試營運，第二廠廠房接近完工，預計年底裝機，明年量產，嘉科二期預計今年底動土。再生水設施規劃尚未成熟，為五個園區中水資源基礎建設最待補強的園區，值得持續追蹤。Chiayi Science Park (CSP), Taiwan's newest science park, primarily uses Lantan and Renyitan Reservoirs (Chiayi City and County), with Nanhua Reservoir available for transfers. Fab 1 is in trial operation; Fab 2 is nearing structural completion (year-end equipment installation, production next year); CSP Phase 2 is expected to break ground by year-end. Reclaimed water planning is still immature—CSP has the least developed water infrastructure of the five parks and warrants ongoing monitoring.
2026 來源：台積電全國建廠大盤點（Vocus）
🇹🇼 高雄科（楠梓）TSMC F22
🌟 高雄F22水資源：全台唯一100%再生水承諾，橋頭＋楠梓兩座再生水廠供水，高屏溪伏流水為備援🌟 Kaohsiung F22 Water: Taiwan's Only 100% Reclaimed Water Commitment; Qiaotou + Nanzih Plants Supply; Gaoping Subsurface Flow as Backup
台積電高雄廠（楠梓）是全台唯一在環評階段即承諾終期製程用水「全量使用再生水」的廠區，不依賴水庫自來水，不排擠農業與民生用水。水源規劃：橋頭再生水廠（已動工，2026年起每日3萬噸）＋楠梓再生水廠（招商中，2028年起每日7萬噸，合計10萬噸/日）。備援水源：高屏溪伏流水（全台最大，最大供水量80萬噸/日）＋荖濃溪伏流水（2026完工）＋抗旱水井。高雄市本身無大型水庫，但高屏溪伏流水資源得天獨厚，每日總用水約163.9萬噸中，伏流水最大可達80萬噸/日。五廠房全面推進中（第1期已裝機、第2期上樑、第3期施工、第4第5期環評通過）。TSMC's Kaohsiung (Nanzih) fab is the only fab in Taiwan that committed during the EIA process to use 100% reclaimed water at full production—without relying on reservoir water and without crowding out agricultural or domestic water. Water plan: Qiaotou reclaimed water plant (under construction, 30K tons/day from 2026) + Nanzih reclaimed water plant (under tender, 70K tons/day from 2028, combined 100K tons/day). Backup: Gaoping subsurface flow water (Taiwan's largest, up to 800K tons/day max), Laonung River subsurface flow (completed 2026), and emergency wells. Kaohsiung has no major reservoir but enjoys abundant subsurface flow from the Gaoping River—the largest watershed in Taiwan. All five fab phases are advancing.

This directly affects all five science parks' new fab planning. Kaohsiung has committed to 100% reclaimed water; HSP's commitment was broken in 2025, sparking the most controversy. President Lai's March announcement on nuclear restart signals an energy policy shift.
