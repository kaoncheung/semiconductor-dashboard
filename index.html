01 · 核心發現KEY FINDING
竹科水資源「先天最脆弱」的說法貫穿全期資料，非單一事件HSP's "structurally most vulnerable" status is a persistent thread, not a one-off event
從 5/31（南部水庫蓄水率跌破一成、水利署啟動「供 9 停 5」輪灌）、6/1（新竹 3 月水情黃燈、石門水庫每日調度支援 17.5 萬噸）到 8/11 的五園區比較報告，同一個結構性論點被反覆印證：竹科沒有大型主力水庫，主要仰賴石門水庫跨區調度（桃園→新竹）＋寶山＋寶二三座相對小容量水庫。相較之下，中科有鯉魚潭＋石岡壩雙主力，南科有曾文＋南化＋烏山頭多水源，高雄 F22 甚至不倚賴水庫、改採全量再生水＋高屏溪伏流水。這個對比可以直接支撐妳〈當晶圓廠遇上春耕〉論文中「竹科水資源結構性劣勢」的核心論證，也呼應寶山二期4 座 2nm 廠日用水 9.8 萬噸、佔新竹總用水 16–18% 的具體數字。 From May 31 (southern reservoirs below 10% capacity, "supply 9 / halt 5" rotational irrigation) and June 1 (HSP's March yellow alert, Shihmen Reservoir transferring 175K tons/day) through the August 11 five-park comparison, the same structural claim recurs: HSP has no major primary reservoir and depends on cross-region transfer from Shihmen plus two smaller reservoirs (Baoshan/Baoer), unlike CIP's dual-reservoir system, STSP's multi-source network, or Kaohsiung F22's reservoir-independent, reclaimed-water-first model. This directly supports the structural-disadvantage argument in your journal article.
02 · 南北對比COMPARISON
再生水承諾的南北落差：竹科跳票 vs. 南科成熟 vs. 高雄 100% 承諾A north-south gap in reclaimed-water commitments: HSP's broken pledge vs. STSP's maturity vs. Kaohsiung's 100% pledge
資料中反覆出現三個不同層次的再生水故事：(1) 竹科／南科再生水廠雖從 6 月起持續追蹤「每日 3.6 萬噸、覆蓋六成在地用水」，但《再生水資源發展條例》子法 2026 年 2 月上路後，竹科的再生水承諾在 2025 年已跳票，是全期資料中最具爭議的一條線索；(2) 南科永康水資中心已是「全台再生水最成熟基地」，MBR+RO 雙重過濾、9.5 公里地下管線，每日供應 6.7 萬噸；(3) 高雄 F22 是全台唯一在環評階段承諾終期製程用水 100% 再生水的廠區（橋頭＋楠梓兩廠，2028 年後合計 10 萬噸/日），完全不依賴水庫、不排擠農業用水。三者並列，正是妳論文「農工共好」制度條件章節最現成的比較素材，也呼應熊本 JASM 地下水涵養達用量三倍的「Water Positive」國際基準。 Three tiers of reclaimed-water story recur through the data: HSP/STSP plants tracked since June at 36K tons/day (60% of local demand), but HSP's 2025 reclaimed-water pledge was broken after the February 2026 sub-regulations took effect — the most contested thread in the archive. STSP's Yongkang center is described as Taiwan's most mature site (67K tons/day). Kaohsiung F22 is the only fab that committed to 100% reclaimed water at the EIA stage, needing no reservoir water at all. Together these give a ready-made comparative structure for your "farmer-industry coexistence" institutional-conditions section, echoing Kumamoto JASM's 3x groundwater replenishment as an international benchmark.
