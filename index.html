01 · 水庫｜結構性劣勢RESERVOIRS
竹科「先天最脆弱」貫穿全期資料，且有具體時間線可追HSP's "structurally most vulnerable" status is documented with a traceable timeline, not a one-off event
五大園區的水庫落差在資料中反覆出現：竹科沒有大型主力水庫，主要仰賴石門水庫跨區調度（桃園→新竹，日常態支援量約 17.5 萬噸/日）＋寶山＋寶二三座相對小容量水庫；中科有鯉魚潭（大安溪，日供水 70 萬噸）＋石岡壩（大甲溪，日供水 70 萬噸）雙主力，合計支應大台中 140 萬噸/日需求；南科有曾文＋南化（2024 年聯通管完工）＋烏山頭＋高屏堰多水源調度；嘉科僅蘭潭＋仁義潭、南化可調度，是五園區中水資源基礎建設最待補強者；高雄 F22 則完全不依賴水庫，改以高屏溪伏流水（全台最大，最大供水量 80 萬噸/日）＋荖濃溪伏流水＋抗旱水井為備援。

時間線上，2026 年 3 月新竹水情曾亮黃燈，同月南部水庫蓄水率一度跌破一成，水利署啟動「供 9 停 5」輪灌，台南新營、嘉義地區農業用水告急；梅雨後（約 5 月）寶山、寶二回復滿庫，顯示竹科水情對降雨的敏感度遠高於其他園區。另一個關鍵數字是竹科 2036 年每日需水量將達 87.94 萬噸，海淡廠與天花湖水庫方案仍在爭議中；寶山二期 4 座 2nm 廠日用水估 9.8 萬噸，佔新竹地區總用水 16–18%，是農業用水衝突最激烈的環節。這條「無主力水庫＋需求持續攀升＋方案未定」的鏈條，可以直接支撐妳論文「竹科水資源結構性劣勢」的核心論證。 HSP has no major primary reservoir, relying on cross-region transfer from Shihmen (~175K tons/day) plus the smaller Baoshan/Baoer reservoirs. CIP has dual sources (Liyutan + Shihgang, 700K tons/day each, serving 1.4M tons/day of Taichung demand); STSP draws from Tsengwen, Nanhua (pipeline-linked since 2024), Wushantou, and Gaoping Weir; Chiayi has only Lantan + Renyitan with Nanhua as backup and is flagged as least-developed; Kaohsiung F22 needs no reservoir at all, relying on Gaoping subsurface flow (up to 800K tons/day) as backup. On the timeline: a March 2026 yellow alert hit Hsinchu, southern reservoirs fell below 10% capacity that same month triggering rotational irrigation, and levels recovered only after the May plum rains — showing HSP's outsized sensitivity to rainfall. HSP's 2036 daily demand is projected at 879.4K tons, with the desalination-plant and Tianhua Lake reservoir options still contested; Baoshan Phase 2's four 2nm fabs alone are estimated at 98K tons/day (16–18% of Hsinchu's total).
02 · 再生水｜南北落差RECLAIMED WATER
竹科跳票 vs. 南科成熟 vs. 高雄 100% 承諾，三個層次的再生水故事Three tiers of reclaimed-water story: HSP's broken pledge, STSP's maturity, Kaohsiung's 100% commitment
《再生水資源發展條例》子法 2026 年 2 月正式上路，規定每日用水逾 2 萬噸的擴建新廠須使用 50% 再生水，直接影響五大園區的新廠規劃——這是整條再生水敘事的制度起點。竹科的再生水承諾在 2025 年已跳票，是資料中最具爭議的一條線索；同時竹科既有的客雅水資中心已超載 110%，二期擴建嚴重落後，2028 年預估將超載到 130%，顯示既有再生水設施本身也撐不住。北部另有桃園龍潭再生水廠規劃，業者希望分階段達到 100% 採用，以及「珍珠串計畫」即將完工、強化跨區供水韌性，這兩項可視為竹科水情的補救路徑。

南科的再生水系統則被資料反覆稱為「全台最成熟」：永康水資源回收中心（MBR+RO 雙重過濾、9.5 公里地下管線）供應台積電、聯電，數字上有兩個版本值得妳交叉查證——6 月多份報告持續引用「每日 3.6 萬噸、覆蓋六成在地用水」，但 8/11 report 給出「每日 6.7 萬噸」，落差近一倍，可能是分期供水量的階段性數字，建議正式引用前回頭查證原始來源（經濟日報／中時的原文）。中科水湳再生水廠是台灣首例三合一（污水＋中水＋再生水）計畫，2024 年底起日供水 1 萬噸，MBR+RO 雙重過濾接近電子級水質，但規模明顯小於南科。

高雄 F22 是全台唯一在環評階段就承諾終期製程用水 100% 再生水的廠區：橋頭再生水廠已動工（2026 年起日供 3 萬噸）＋楠梓再生水廠招商中（2028 年起日供 7 萬噸），合計 10 萬噸/日，完全不依賴水庫自來水、不排擠農業與民生用水。這三層對比（跳票／成熟／100% 承諾）正是妳論文「農工共好」制度條件章節最現成的比較素材，也呼應熊本 JASM 地下水涵養達用量三倍的「Water Positive」國際基準，以及美國 Amazon 資料中心「water positive」目標已達成 75% 的另一個可引用對照案例。 The Reclaimed Water Act sub-regulations (effective Feb 2026) require large expansions (20K+ tons/day) to use 50% reclaimed water — the regulatory starting point for the whole narrative. HSP's own reclaimed-water pledge was broken in 2025, and its existing Keya Water Resource Center is already overloaded at 110% (projected 130% by 2028), meaning even the fallback infrastructure is strained. STSP's Yongkang Center is called Taiwan's most mature reclaimed-water site, though reported daily volumes vary between sources (36K tons/day repeated through June vs. 67K tons/day in the Aug 11 report) — worth cross-checking the original Chinese-language sources before citing either figure. CIP's Shuinan plant, Taiwan's first triple-integrated (sewage + graywater + reclaimed) project, supplies a much smaller 10K tons/day since late 2024. Kaohsiung F22 remains the standout: the only fab to commit to 100% reclaimed water at the EIA stage, combining Qiaotou (30K tons/day, under construction) and Nanzih (70K tons/day, tendering) plants for 100K tons/day total by 2028, with zero reservoir dependence.
03 · 電力｜平行壓力POWER
電力議題在 6–7 月急速膨脹，台灣本地數字與國際電網危機交織Power coverage exploded in June–July, weaving Taiwan-specific figures with the international grid crisis
分類條目統計顯示水資源（91 則）與電力（83 則）＋能源政策（82 則）數量相近，但時序分布明顯不同：水資源條目集中在 5/31–6/13 與 8/11 兩端，中段（6/15–7/17）大量條目其實是美國 PJM 電網危機、熱浪、DOE 緊急命令的國際新聞（幾乎每份報告都有 4–5 則），起因是美國東岸資料中心用電暴增——PJM 尖峰需求一路衝到 166,147 MW（改寫 2006 年以來紀錄）、北維吉尼亞電力現貨價飆破 2,000 美元/MWh（平時 50 倍）、DOE 兩度發布緊急命令允許限縮資料中心用電。這波國際危機本質上跟台灣半導體擴產的用電壓力是同一種結構（AI 運算需求推升用電），可以作為妳論文比較框架的國際對照組。

台灣本地的關鍵數字：台電估 2026–2030 年新增用電逾 500 萬瓩（5GW，年均增量是過去 10 年 2 倍以上）；台積電 2030 年用電估占全台 15.6%，備轉容量率尖峰恐低於 6%；到 2035 年台灣半導體用電將倍增至 1,100 億度，2028–29 年備轉容量率恐降至 7–8%。TSIA（台灣半導體產業協會）曾罕見發出公開警告，指電力短缺已威脅台灣的半導體優勢；台積電因應之道是 A14 製程目標每一代降耗 30%，以技術創新對沖電力風險。地區對比上，高雄本身發電量充足，甚至可「南電北送」支援其他縣市，電力條件遠優於新竹；但楠梓科學園區同時因年耗電量預估達 112 億度（逼近高雄全市用電量）引發社區強烈反彈，顯示高雄雖然電力供給結構較佳，開發過程的社會接受度爭議並未比竹科小。竹科自身則有 3 月竹園超高壓變電所火警（1 死 2 傷）的供電韌性事件，以及 7 月巴威颱風造成全台 24 萬戶停電（台電跨區搶修復電逾 95%）。這批電力資料建議留給博論後續章節或另立短論，避免期刊論文範疇失焦，但作為「資源治理的雙重擠壓」敘事的輔助材料相當完整。 Water (91) and power (83) + energy policy (82) items are similar in volume but diverge sharply in timing: water clusters at the two ends of the period, while mid-period coverage (Jun 15–Jul 17) is dominated by the US PJM grid crisis — peak demand hitting 166,147 MW (a record since 2006), Northern Virginia spot prices spiking past $2,000/MWh (50x normal), and DOE issuing emergency orders to curtail data-center load twice. This international AI-driven power crunch mirrors Taiwan's own semiconductor-driven power pressure and makes a useful international comparison. On the Taiwan side: Taipower projects +5GW of new demand 2026–2030 (double the past decade's annual pace); TSMC's power use could reach 15.6% of the national total by 2030 with peak reserve margins possibly below 6%; and by 2035 Taiwan's semiconductor power use is projected to double to 110 billion kWh, with reserve margins potentially falling to 7–8% in 2028–29. TSIA issued a rare public warning that power shortages threaten Taiwan's semiconductor edge. Kaohsiung has surplus generation capacity ("south power north supply") but the Nanzih park's projected annual consumption (11.2 billion kWh, near Kaohsiung city's entire usage) has triggered strong community pushback — showing that better power supply doesn't eliminate social friction. HSP itself saw a fatal March substation fire and 240K households lose power in a July typhoon.
